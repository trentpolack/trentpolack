# Hiya, I’m Trent Polack!
I make games, build tools, and spend an unreasonable amount of time thinking about how things interact.

I’m a gameplay/systems/technical designer and programmer with 20+ years of experience across indie games, AAA development, educational technology, and other ventures. I've worked at **CD PROJEKT RED**, **Housemarque**, **Arkane Studios**, **LightBox Interactive**, and more.

I like games that give players room to experiment, improvise, and be the best little agents of chaos they can be. My favorite work connects the underlying technology to that freedom: flexible gameplay systems, procedural worlds, and tools that make trying something strange a little easier.

## What I’m building
- **[Joy Engine](https://github.com/trentpolack/joy-engine)**: My playground for engine development, rendering, and systems-driven game experiments.
- **[FORM LAB](https://github.com/trentpolack/joy-engine/tree/develop/editor/tools/form-lab)**: script-driven procedural geometry and tools for turning an idea into something you can actually play with.
- **[PARTICLE LAB](https://github.com/trentpolack/joy-engine/tree/develop/editor/tools/particle-lab)**: particle authoring, live previews, and scriptable effects. Some experiments need sparks.
- And more...

All of this is to support design and development with less friction between the high concept and the playable thing.
